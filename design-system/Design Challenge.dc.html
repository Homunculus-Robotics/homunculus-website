02 — How it runs

Open to anyone. Judged by everyone.

01

What to send

Renders, sketches, a short video, a 3D model, or a written concept. One page of reasoning beats ten glamour shots — tell us what the body is for.

02

How voting works

Everyone gets a fixed budget of tokens and spends them one per design. No score inflation, no vote farming — a token you give is a token you no longer have.

03

What you get

The most-backed entries get built in the sandbox as working morphologies, and shown at the next Robotics Collective night. Details to be confirmed.

Founder portrait — 8 treatments

No green wash.

Same crop, eight ways to sit it in the page: A–D on look, E–H borrowed from simulation. Tell me a letter and I'll drop it into About.

A — Plain silver

Clean black-and-white, neutral hairline, no tint at all. The photo carries itself; the page stays the loud one.

B — CRT scanline

Monochrome under a fine scanline grid with a slow sweep. Reads as a monitor feed, matches the phosphor type without colouring skin.

C — Perception overlay

Photo stays in natural colour; the robotics idea lives in the violet detection box and readouts on top of it.

D — Mounted on grid

Full colour, nothing over the face. The photo sits inside a blueprint-grid plate with the caption outside the frame.

Round 2 — simulation

Four more, each borrowing something from how a sim actually looks.

E — Point cloud

The face resolved out of a dot lattice, like a depth scan before it's meshed. Skin tone drops out entirely, so nothing can look off-colour.

F — Digital twin

Photo on the left, its simulated copy on the right, one hairline between them. The sim-to-real story told on the founder himself.

G — Sim viewport

Framed like a running simulation window — file name up top, step counter and gravity in the corner. The chrome does the work, the photo stays untouched.

H — Randomized runs

One hero frame plus three variants under different lighting, the way domain randomization spits out the same scene many times.
